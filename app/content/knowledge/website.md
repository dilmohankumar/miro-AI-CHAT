# How claiming #1 works

## Steps
1. Click "Claim #1" on the main page.
2. Fill in the form (one time): website/business name, website URL, optional logo, and a short description. Click "Continue to payment".
3. The "Pay to become #1" screen shows your listing preview and the exact amount. Click "Pay".
4. Pay on the secure checkout. You become #1 the moment the payment is confirmed. A "You're #1" success screen shows your new throne card.

There is no account, login or signup. Anyone can claim.

## Price rules (server decides, never the browser)
- If the board is empty, the first claim costs ₹1,000 (minimum entry amount).
- Otherwise the price to take #1 = what the current #1 paid + ₹1,000 (a flat step-up, not doubling). Example: #1 paid ₹5,000 -> the next claim costs ₹6,000.
- The pay screen tells you how much more it is than the current #1 paid.
- Currency is Indian rupees (INR). It is a one-time payment, not a subscription.
- When you start paying, that price is locked for you for 15 minutes. If the order is cancelled, the window is closed or 15 minutes pass without payment, the price is released again.
- Two people can never be quoted the same price at the same time.

## Form rules
- Website / business name: required, up to 40 characters in the form (e.g. "Meera's Bakery").
- Website URL: required, e.g. meerasbakery.in (up to 200 characters). It is shown as a link that opens in a new tab.
- Logo: optional. Upload an image (png, jpg, webp, gif or svg), up to about 500 KB. If no logo is given, the first letter of the name is shown instead.
- Description: required, up to 140 characters. This is what people will read under your name.

## Payment
- Payments are handled by Razorpay (UPI, card, netbanking) and also by Stripe (card payment form on the page). Pay Later and EMI are not offered.
- Card and UPI details are entered on the payment provider's secure checkout. AkatuskiSites never sees or stores payment details.
- If a Razorpay payment is declined, its window stays open and shows the reason and a "Retry payment" option. Closing that window cancels the order and releases the price.
- If you pay by Stripe and return to the site, you may see: "Confirming your payment…", "Payment received, finishing up" (your spot will update shortly, you can leave the page), "Payment cancelled" (you were not charged) or "Payment not completed" (you were not charged).
- Leaving the page without paying does not charge you.

## Ranking and the Top 10
- Ranking is only by amount, highest first. If two amounts are equal, the one paid earlier ranks higher.
- Every claim costs more than the current #1, so a successful claim always becomes #1 and everyone else moves down one place. You cannot buy rank #2, #5 etc. directly.
- Only the top 10 are shown. A business pushed below rank 10 leaves the visible board; its record is kept in an archive, not deleted.
- You stay #1 until someone pays more than your amount. Anyone, including a business that lost the spot, can take it back by paying more.

## Support
- Click "support" in the footer. The form asks: your name, email, "What's this about" (subject, 3 to 140 characters) and details (up to 2000 characters, optional).
- You get a reference code like VRT-48213 right away, and the team replies by email within 24 hours.
- Sending the same ticket twice within a minute returns the same reference code. Too many tickets or too many checkout attempts in 10 minutes will show a "try again later" message.
