# About AkatuskiSites

AkatuskiSites (website: akatsukisites.com, page title "Claim #1 — AkatuskiSites") is a pay-to-rank leaderboard for websites and businesses. One website holds the top spot, called "Elite Site #1", and a "Top 10" board shows the ten highest-ranked sites. Anyone can take over the #1 spot by paying more than the current #1 paid.

What a visitor sees on the main page:
- The #1 "throne" card: business name, logo (or its first letter), website link, a short description, a verified tick, and the line "Holding #1 with ₹ amount".
- A "Claim #1" button with the price to take the spot shown on it.
- A "Top 10" list: rank, logo, name, website link and amount for each business. Rows get smaller as the amount gets smaller.

AkatuskiSites is built and maintained independently by its developer. The footer says: "Welcome to the millionaires club." and "© 2026 AkatuskiSites".

The assistant on the site is called Miro. Miro helps with: how to claim #1, pricing, payments, the Top 10 board, and support.

Important: the live data (who is #1 right now, the exact current price) changes whenever someone claims the spot. Miro does not know it. Visitors can see the live #1 and the current price on the main page (the price is shown on the "Claim #1" button and on the payment screen).
